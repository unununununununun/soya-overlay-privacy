Soya Overlay Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Soya Overlay app for Android (package com.soyaoverlay.app), together with any related services operated by Kiryl Zmitrovich (collectively, the “Application”). Kiryl Zmitrovich is hereby referred to as the “Service Provider”.

Information Collection and Use

Soya Overlay is an on-device overlay. It covers spots the user chooses (for example a talking-head or a banner) in apps the user picks. Most data stays on the device.

The Application stores on the device:

  • Overlay settings: selected app package names, position, size, color, opacity, and optional image or GIF files the user picks for the cover
  • Language and light/dark theme preference
  • A local cache of whether Pro was purchased

With permissions the user grants:

  • Appear on top of other apps — to draw the cover over the chosen app. No data is sent off the device for this.
  • Usage access — to read which app is in the foreground so the cover is shown only there. Package names are processed on the device and are not uploaded to the Service Provider.
  • Notifications — for the persistent notification that keeps the overlay service running.
  • Accounts — Google account addresses on the device may be read locally to check a Pro unlock. Addresses are not uploaded to the Service Provider’s servers.
  • Screen capture (MediaProjection) — declared so a region under the overlay can be blurred. That fill is currently disabled in the UI. If it is used, captured frames stay on the device and are not uploaded.

The Application does not run ads. The Service Provider does not operate an analytics or crash service in this app, and does not sell personal information.

The Service Provider does not require you to create an account with the Service Provider. Pro is a one-time Google Play purchase.

Cookies and tracking technologies

The Application does not use cookies. Google Play Billing and RevenueCat may use identifiers required to process a purchase (for example an anonymous app user id and purchase tokens).

Third Party Access

The Application uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services / Google Play Billing
  • RevenueCat (entitlement for the one-time Pro purchase)

If Android backup is enabled on the device, Google may back up app settings with the user’s Google account under Google’s policy.

The Service Provider may disclose information:

  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

International Data Transfers

Purchase processing (Google and RevenueCat) may take place outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area. Those providers apply their own transfer safeguards.
